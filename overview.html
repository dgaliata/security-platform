CI/CD Gate Semgrep detects a missing auth check in the PR. Build is failed; results are automatically synced to DefectDojo.

Supply Chain
4

Runtime
5
Automated Response Tines SOAR playbook triggers: Isolates the network segment, captures Pod logs, and alerts the On-Call Engineer.
Response
